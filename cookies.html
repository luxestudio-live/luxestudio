Cookie & Privacy Protocols

LuxeStudio operates on strict DPDP data ethics. We deploy zero advertising cookies and zero cross-site trackers. Performance and UX diagnostics (Microsoft Clarity) are activated strictly upon your affirmative consent.

SECTION INDEX
01.0 Zero Ad Cookies 02.0 Client Preferences 03.0 UX Diagnostics (Clarity) 04.0 Third-Party Processors 05.0 Interactive Consent Hub
SECTION 01.0

Zero Advertising Cookies & DPDP Architecture

LuxeStudio adheres strictly to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP) and modern global privacy standards. We operate on a clean digital hygiene principle:

Zero Ad Tracking: We never set third-party advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, or behavioral profiling beacons. We do not monetize browsing telemetry or sell visitor data under any circumstances.

Any non-essential cookies (such as performance and user experience analytics) are strictly consent-gated. They will never be written to your browser unless you grant explicit affirmative consent via our consent prompt.

SECTION 02.0

Client-Side State & Functional Storage

Our platform respects your hardware preferences and local autonomy:

  • Reduced Motion: Detected dynamically via the CSS prefers-reduced-motion media query. We do not write tracking tokens to your machine.
  • Consent Memory (LocalStorage): We store your consent decision under the key luxestudio_cookie_consent (value: granted or denied) so that we do not repeatedly disrupt your browsing.
  • Ephemeral Session Memory: Interactive interface states (such as active navigation drawers and accordion toggles) exist only in memory during your active session.
SECTION 03.0

UX Diagnostics & Telemetry (Microsoft Clarity)

To identify UI layout shifts, diagnose broken links, and optimize website responsiveness, LuxeStudio utilizes Microsoft Clarity. In full compliance with DPDP Section 6, this service is only loaded if you explicitly accept telemetry.

When consent is granted, the following first-party telemetry cookies may be set:

  • _clck: Persists the unique anonymous Clarity user ID across page views to understand visitor flows (Expires: 1 year).
  • _clsk: Connects multiple page views in a single session into unified playback telemetry (Expires: 1 day).
  • CLID: Identifies the first-time browser instance for performance deduplication.
Privacy Safeguards & Masking: Sensitive input fields and form keystrokes are automatically masked by default. Microsoft Clarity never captures financial details, credentials, or personal secrets, and data is never utilized for targeted advertising.
SECTION 04.0

Third-Party Services & External Integrations

LuxeStudio integrates with trusted technical processors operating under robust security certifications (ISO 27001, SOC 2):

  • Content Delivery & Fonts: Google Fonts and GSAP animations are delivered over encrypted HTTPS without advertising cookies.
  • External Booking & Messaging: If you book a discovery call via Calendly or message our studio via WhatsApp, you navigate directly to their respective domains (calendly.com or wa.me), which operate under their own independent privacy notices.
  • Zero-Backend Form Dispatch: Discovery briefs submitted via our contact page are transmitted over TLS via FormSubmit directly to studio management mailboxes.
SECTION 05.0

Consent Management Hub (DPDP Section 6(4))

Under Section 6(4) of the DPDP Act 2023, you have the right to withdraw or modify your consent at any time, with the same ease as granting it. Use our interactive consent controls below:

YOUR CURRENT PREFERENCE: CHECKING...

Click below to open the consent prompt and update your preference, or immediately revoke telemetry authorization across your browser.

Browser-Level Blocking: You can also block or clear all cookies at any time via your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, or Brave). Blocking analytics cookies will have zero impact on the visual quality or functionality of LuxeStudio.
