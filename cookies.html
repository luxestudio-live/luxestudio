LEGAL // 03.0 ZERO-COOKIE ARCHITECTURE

Cookie & Privacy Protocols

LuxeStudio operates on a zero-bloat standard. We do not set tracking cookies, write persistent profiling tokens to your device, or sell behavioral browsing data.

SECTION INDEX
01.0 Zero-Cookie Standard 02.0 Client-Side State 03.0 Third-Party Services 04.0 Zero Ad Trackers 05.0 Browser Controls
SECTION 01.0

Zero-Cookie Standard

Unlike conventional digital agencies that clutter your browser with analytics beacons, cross-site identifiers, and behavioral trackers, LuxeStudio is engineered to be 100% cookie-free on our primary domain. We do not set any first-party cookies when you navigate our portfolio, read our case studies, or view our services.

SECTION 02.0

Client-Side State & Preferences

Our platform respects your hardware and accessibility configurations without saving tracking tokens:

  • Reduced Motion: We detect your operating system's motion preference in real time via CSS prefers-reduced-motion media queries. We do not write persistent tracking tokens or storage keys to your machine.
  • Ephemeral Memory: Interactive interface states (such as active navigation drawers and accordion accord toggles) exist only in your browser's active tab memory and are immediately cleared when you close the tab.
SECTION 03.0

Third-Party Services & External Integrations

To deliver peak performance, typographic fidelity, and project inquiry channels, LuxeStudio interacts with select third-party services:

  • Content Delivery & Fonts: Typography (Google Fonts) and core animation frameworks (GSAP, Lenis) are loaded over encrypted HTTPS connections. These providers deliver static asset files without placing advertising cookies.
  • External Booking & Messaging: When you choose to book a discovery call via Calendly or start a conversation via WhatsApp, you navigate directly to their respective domains (calendly.com or wa.me), which operate under their own independent privacy and cookie policies.
  • Zero-Backend Form Dispatch: Inquiries submitted via our contact brief are transmitted directly to our secure email routing partner (FormSubmit) over TLS to deliver your message to our studio inbox without persistent behavioral profiling.
SECTION 04.0

Zero Third-Party Advertising Trackers

Clean Digital Hygiene: LuxeStudio maintains zero integration with advertising networks. We do not load Meta Pixels, Google Tag Manager remarketing tags, TikTok tracking pixels, or cross-site fingerprinting scripts.
SECTION 05.0

Managing Storage & Cookie Preferences

Because LuxeStudio does not deploy tracking cookies, you do not need to manage complex consent banners or opt-out forms to enjoy a private experience. You can independently inspect, block, or clear storage at any time through your browser's security preferences (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, or Brave).
