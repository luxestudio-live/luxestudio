LEGAL // 02.0 DATA INTEGRITY & ETHICS

Privacy & Data Ethics

We believe privacy is an architectural principle, not an afterthought. We do not sell user data, run invasive trackers, or monetize visitor telemetry.

SECTION INDEX
01.0 Information Collected 02.0 Zero-Surveillance Pledge 03.0 Technical Infrastructure 04.0 Retention & Security 05.0 Global User Rights 06.0 Data Officer Inquiries
SECTION 01.0

Information We Collect

LuxeStudio collects only the minimum information necessary to conduct project consultations, execute engineering agreements, and ensure website performance:

  • Inquiry Brief Data: Name, professional email address, company name, project parameters, and notes submitted via our discovery form.
  • Technical Telemetry: Aggregated anonymous network performance metrics (TTFB, FCP, Core Web Vitals) through edge CDN routing.
SECTION 02.0

Zero-Surveillance Pledge

No Data Brokering: LuxeStudio will never sell, lease, rent, or trade client information to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.

We do not employ cross-site tracking pixels, persistent social tracking beacons, or fingerprinting scripts. Your interaction on LuxeStudio remains isolated and confidential.

SECTION 03.0

Technical Infrastructure & Processors

To deliver low-latency global experiences, we utilize industry-leading cloud infrastructure providers who comply with ISO 27001, SOC 2, and GDPR standards (e.g., Cloudflare CDN, Vercel Edge Network, GitHub Pages, Google Cloud Fonts).

SECTION 04.0

Data Retention & Security Standards

Inquiry information is stored in encrypted databases with strict multi-factor access control. Project data is retained for the duration of the commercial relationship and archived securely thereafter in accordance with statutory accounting obligations.

SECTION 05.0

Global User Rights (GDPR, CCPA & DPDP)

Regardless of geographic location, all visitors enjoy full sovereignty over their data, including:

  • Right of Access & Portability: Request a complete copy of your submitted data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request immediate permanent deletion of your project inquiries.
  • Right of Rectification: Update inaccurate business parameters at any time.
