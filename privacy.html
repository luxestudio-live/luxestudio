LEGAL // 02.0 DATA INTEGRITY & ETHICS

Privacy & Data Ethics

We believe privacy is an architectural principle, not an afterthought. We do not sell user data, run invasive trackers, or monetize visitor telemetry.

SECTION INDEX
01.0 Information Collected 02.0 Zero-Surveillance Pledge 03.0 Technical Processors 04.0 Retention & Security 05.0 DPDP & Global Rights 06.0 Protection of Minors 07.0 Grievance Officer & DPBI
SECTION 01.0

Information We Collect & Lawful Grounds

LuxeStudio collects only the minimum personal data necessary to evaluate commercial inquiries, conduct project consultations, execute engineering agreements, and ensure platform performance. All data is processed pursuant to Section 4 and Section 6 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP) and global data privacy standards:

  • Inquiry Brief Data: Full Name, telephone/WhatsApp contact number, professional email address, and project requirements voluntarily provided via our discovery form with affirmative consent.
  • Consent-Gated UX Telemetry: Anonymized behavioral telemetry (clicks, heatmaps, scroll depth, interaction friction) via Microsoft Clarity. This is only activated after you provide affirmative consent via our DPDP consent dialog. Keystroke masking is strictly enforced.
  • Edge Network Telemetry: Anonymous edge CDN technical diagnostics (TTFB, FCP, latency metrics) strictly necessary for cyber defense and network load distribution.
SECTION 02.0

Zero-Surveillance & No Advertising Pledge

No Data Brokering or Ad Tracking: LuxeStudio will never sell, lease, monetize, or trade client or visitor data to third-party data brokers, ad networks, or marketing consortia.

We do not employ cross-site tracking pixels (such as Meta Pixel or TikTok Pixel), persistent profiling identifiers, or canvas fingerprinting. Your interaction on LuxeStudio remains confidential and contained within our domain.

SECTION 03.0

Technical Infrastructure & Data Processors

In our capacity as Data Fiduciary, LuxeStudio engages only vetted, compliant technical Data Processors bound by strict confidentiality and data protection obligations under DPDP Section 8(2):

  • Cloud Hosting & Edge Delivery: AWS Amplify & Cloudflare CDN (Global TLS edge caching, SOC 2, ISO 27001).
  • Encrypted Form Transmission: FormSubmit (Zero-backend TLS relay to designated studio partner mailboxes for project evaluation).
  • UX Diagnostics (Consent-Gated): Microsoft Clarity (Azure cloud infrastructure; GDPR and DPDP compliant telemetry).
  • Typographic Delivery: Google Fonts (Static asset caching with zero tracking cookies).
SECTION 04.0

Data Retention & Security Safeguards

Under Section 8(5) of the DPDP Act 2023, LuxeStudio implements robust technical and organizational security safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or personal data breach:

  • Transport Layer Encryption: All transit occurs over TLS 1.3 encryption with strict HSTS enforcement.
  • Purpose-Bound Retention: Contact brief inquiries are retained solely for the lifecycle of consultation and commercial collaboration, after which they are securely expunged, unless retention is required under statutory accounting laws.
  • Breach Notification: In the unforeseen event of a personal data breach, LuxeStudio will notify the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) and affected Data Principals in accordance with statutory requirements.
SECTION 05.0

Data Principal Rights (DPDP Act 2023 & Global Frameworks)

As a Data Principal under India's DPDP Act 2023 (and global regulations such as GDPR / CCPA), you are vested with full enforceable rights over your digital personal data:

  • Right to Access Information (Section 11): Request a summary of personal data processed, identities of data processors involved, and any relevant processing details.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure (Section 12): Request correction of inaccurate information, completion of incomplete data, or permanent erasure of personal data that has fulfilled its specified purpose.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13): Access a structured, readily available grievance redressal mechanism provided by LuxeStudio.
  • Right to Nominate (Section 14): Designate another individual who shall exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Section 6(4)): Withdraw consent at any time with the same ease as granting it. You can manage your telemetry preferences anytime on our Cookie Protocol page.
SECTION 06.0

Protection of Children's Personal Data

In strict observance of Section 9 of the DPDP Act 2023, LuxeStudio provides bespoke enterprise, design, and software engineering services intended solely for individuals aged eighteen (18) and older. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal data belonging to children, nor do we deploy behavioral monitoring or targeted profiling directed at minors.

SECTION 07.0

Data Protection Officer & Grievance Redressal

In compliance with Section 13 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, LuxeStudio has appointed a dedicated Grievance Redressal Officer to address inquiries and rights requests:

DATA FIDUCIARY: LuxeStudio Creative Technology
DESIGNATED GRIEVANCE OFFICER: Office of the Data Protection & Grievance Officer
DIRECT GRIEVANCE EMAIL: grievance@theluxestudio.in · privacy@theluxestudio.in
LOCATION & JURISDICTION: Mumbai, Maharashtra 400001, India
STATUTORY RESOLUTION TIMELINE: Grievance acknowledgment within 48 hours; full resolution within 30 calendar days.

Escalation to the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI): If your grievance is not resolved to your satisfaction within thirty (30) days, or if you believe our data processing violates the DPDP Act 2023, you have the statutory right under Section 13(3) of the Act to lodge a formal complaint with the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI).
