FAQ / BEFORE WE BUILD STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Everything you need
to know before we begin.

Clear answers on what we build, how projects start, timelines, costs, automation, and what working with LuxeStudio actually looks like.

01 What does LuxeStudio actually build? +

We design and build digital experiences, websites, custom software, automated workflows, integrations, and digital systems.

Some projects start with a website. Others start with a business problem, a manual process, or a system that has stopped scaling. We work from the problem backward and build what actually needs to exist.

02 Do you only build websites? +

No.

Websites are one part of what we do. We also build custom digital experiences, internal tools, workflow systems, automations, integrations, and bespoke development.

If something repetitive, disconnected, or unnecessarily complicated is slowing the business down, there's usually a better way to build it.

03 Can you work with our existing website or system? +

Yes.

You don't always need to start from zero. We can work with an existing website, application, API, CMS, database, or workflow — whether that means improving what's already there, connecting disconnected systems, or rebuilding a part that has outgrown its original architecture.

04 How does a project with LuxeStudio begin? +

With a conversation.

You tell us what you're trying to achieve, what's currently getting in the way, and what you want to change. We look at the problem, understand the requirements, and determine what should actually be built before jumping into design or development.

No unnecessary solution is prescribed before the problem is understood.

05 How long does a project take? +

It depends on what we're building.

A focused website and a custom digital system have very different timelines. Once we understand the scope, we'll outline the major phases, dependencies, and expected timeline before development begins.

We'd rather give you a realistic timeline than an impressive one.

06 How much does a project cost? +

There isn't one fixed LuxeStudio price because there isn't one fixed type of project.

Cost depends on scope, complexity, integrations, functionality, content, and the level of custom development involved. After understanding the project, we'll provide a clear scope and estimate before work begins.

07 Do you work with businesses outside India? +

Yes.

Digital products don't need to be built in the same room — or the same country. We can work remotely with businesses and teams across different locations using structured communication, shared documentation, and collaborative tools.

08 Can you automate processes we currently do manually? +

Yes.

We can map an existing workflow, identify repetitive steps, connect the systems involved, and automate the parts that shouldn't require constant human effort.

The goal isn't to automate everything. It's to remove the work that doesn't need to be done manually.

09 Can LuxeStudio connect different tools and platforms? +

Yes.

Where APIs, webhooks, integrations, or other supported methods are available, we can connect the systems your business already uses. For example:

  • CRM → Website
  • Forms → Database
  • Website → Internal tools
  • Payments → Operations
  • Multiple platforms → One workflow

The exact approach depends on the systems involved.

10 Will I be involved during the project? +

Yes — but you won't need to manage the project yourself.

We keep communication focused around important decisions, feedback, milestones, and approvals. Our job is to handle the complexity while keeping you close enough to know exactly where things stand.

11 What happens if I already have a design? +

That's completely fine.

We can work from an existing design system, improve an existing direction, or take responsibility for the entire process from strategy and design through development. The right level of involvement depends on what the project needs.

12 Do you provide ongoing support after launch? +

Yes, when the project requires it.

Depending on the engagement, we can continue with maintenance, improvements, new functionality, technical support, or further development after launch. A launch doesn't necessarily have to be the end of the relationship.

13 Who owns the website and code after the project? +

The ownership and handover terms are defined as part of the project agreement.

Our aim is to leave you with a usable, maintainable product and the appropriate project assets and access agreed upon for the engagement.

14 What do you need from us to get started? +

Usually, a clear understanding of:

  • What you're trying to achieve
  • What's currently not working
  • Who the product is for
  • Existing systems or tools involved
  • Important requirements or constraints
  • Your desired timeline

You don't need to have everything figured out before contacting us. That's part of what the first conversation is for.

15 What if I'm not sure exactly what I need? +

That's okay.

You don't need to arrive with a technical specification. Tell us what you're trying to accomplish, what's frustrating you, or where you think there's an opportunity.

We'll help turn that into something concrete.
