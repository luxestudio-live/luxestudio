COMMERCE FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

01 How does LuxeStudio approach headless e-commerce differently than standard Shopify themes? +

Standard Shopify themes rely on heavy liquid templates and dozens of third-party apps that slow down mobile checkout. We decouple the front end using high-speed Next.js and custom CSS, communicating directly with Shopify's Storefront GraphQL API. This results in sub-second page transitions, instant micro-cart interactions, and zero layout shifts.

02 Can you build high-converting editorial lookbooks and micro-carts for luxury fashion? +

Yes. For luxury fashion labels like Luxxe Labels, we build editorial collection lookbooks, fluid category filtering, slide-out micro-carts, and direct WhatsApp VIP stylist integration, delivering sub-second shopping experiences that elevate brand prestige.

03 How do you optimize luxury e-commerce platforms for higher Average Order Value (AOV)? +

Through editorial lookbook storytelling, frictionless sliding micro-carts, intelligent bundle recommendations, and high-resolution material zoom that gives discerning buyers confidence in premium product quality.

04 What payment gateways and international currencies do you support? +

We support global payment processing via Stripe, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Razorpay, and PayPal with automated multi-currency geolocation and zero-fraud verification webhooks.
