FREQUENTLY ASKED

Website Redesign FAQs

01 Will our website lose existing Google rankings during a redesign? +

No. We implement an exhaustive 1-to-1 301 URL redirect mapping, maintain existing metadata equity, preserve key heading hierarchies, and eliminate crawl errors. In fact, most clients see an immediate ranking boost because the redesigned site achieves 98+ Google Core Web Vitals.

02 How do you handle migrating our existing blog posts and case studies? +

We programmatically export and clean your existing content database, convert images into modern WebP/AVIF formats, optimize internal link structures, and migrate the data seamlessly into a clean, modern CMS architecture.

03 How do we know if our website actually needs a redesign? +

Key symptoms that necessitate a redesign include: mobile load times over 3 seconds, low mobile visitor conversion rates, outdated visual aesthetics compared to newer competitors, and an inability to easily update lead forms or connect with modern CRMs.

04 How long does a full website redesign take? +

A comprehensive redesign typically takes 3 to 5 weeks from initial SEO forensic audit and Figma prototyping to production staging and final DNS cutover.
