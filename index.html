08 / Intel Frequently Asked Questions

Clarity before
commitment.

Straight answers before we begin. What we build, how we work, and what to expect.

Have a specific question? Start a conversation ↗
01 What does LuxeStudio actually build? +

We design and build digital experiences, websites, custom software, automated workflows, integrations, and digital systems. Some projects start with a website. Others start with a business problem, a manual process, or a system that has stopped scaling. We work from the problem backward and build what actually needs to exist.

02 Do you only build websites? +

No. Websites are one part of what we do. We also build custom digital experiences, internal tools, workflow systems, automations, integrations, and bespoke development. If something repetitive, disconnected, or unnecessarily complicated is slowing the business down, there's usually a better way to build it.

03 How does a project with LuxeStudio begin? +

With a conversation. You tell us what you're trying to achieve, what's currently getting in the way, and what you want to change. We look at the problem, understand the requirements, and determine what should actually be built before jumping into design or development. No unnecessary solution is prescribed before the problem is understood.

04 How long does a project take? +

It depends on what we're building. A focused website and a custom digital system have very different timelines. Once we understand the scope, we'll outline the major phases, dependencies, and expected timeline before development begins. We'd rather give you a realistic timeline than an impressive one.

05 Can you automate processes we currently do manually? +

Yes. We can map an existing workflow, identify repetitive steps, connect the systems involved, and automate the parts that shouldn't require constant human effort. The goal isn't to automate everything — it's to remove the work that doesn't need to be done manually.

06 What if I'm not sure exactly what I need? +

That's okay. You don't need to arrive with a technical specification. Tell us what you're trying to accomplish, what's frustrating you, or where you think there's an opportunity. We'll help turn that into something concrete.
