SPATIAL FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

01 How does LuxeStudio stream heavy 4K architectural renders without slowing down the site? +

We build custom automated image optimization pipelines that encode assets into modern AVIF and WebP formats with fluid responsive srcsets. Assets are progressively decoded offscreen using intersection observers, maintaining sub-second paints even on mobile networks.

02 Can you build interactive 3D WebGL or architectural lighting simulations in the browser? +

Yes. As demonstrated with Auri Concept, we write custom Three.js and GLSL shaders that render photorealistic beam-angles, material reflections, and spatial dimensions at a locked 60 frames per second without requiring any plugins.

03 How do you help architecture firms capture high-net-worth residential and commercial inquiries? +

We structure high-ticket conversion pathways: editorial project lookbooks that lead into gated architectural specification downloads, direct consultation calendars, and private client presentation vaults.

04 How long does an architectural portfolio build take? +

A flagship architectural studio build typically takes 4 to 6 weeks, structured across spatial narrative design, WebGL prototyping, asset optimization, and CDN edge deployment.
