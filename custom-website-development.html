COMMONLY ASKED

Frequently Asked Questions

01 Why choose custom website development over WordPress, Wix, or Webflow? +

Generic website builders and WordPress themes rely on dozens of third-party plugins and heavy stylesheet bloat, causing slow 3–6 second load times and failed Core Web Vitals. LuxeStudio writes clean, handcrafted code from first principles, delivering sub-second speeds (0.3s–0.8s), perfect 98+ Lighthouse scores, custom brand typography, and complete ownership of your intellectual property.

02 Do we own 100% of the website code and assets after launch? +

Yes, absolutely. Unlike proprietary platforms that lock you into monthly hosting fees or closed ecosystems, LuxeStudio provides complete ownership of all repository code, assets, and design files with zero vendor lock-in.

03 What tech stack does LuxeStudio use for custom website development? +

We engineer frontend experiences using handcrafted semantic HTML5, Vanilla CSS3, modern ES6+ JavaScript, Next.js for static edge deployments, GSAP for smooth motion choreographies, and Three.js/WebGL for immersive spatial experiences.

04 How long does a bespoke website development sprint take? +

A focused bespoke website or flagship brand portal typically takes 3 to 5 weeks from architecture kickoff to production launch. Complex builds with 3D WebGL simulations or custom backend workflows take 5 to 7 weeks.
